Her story

Why Dexter Sports Co. exists

Kim's understanding of athletes starts on the court. A Charlotte native, she began her collegiate volleyball career at the junior-college level before earning her place on the Division I roster at Howard University. That path taught her what the recruiting process, a transfer, and the balance of classes, competition, and recovery really ask of a student-athlete and their family.

Off the court, Kim built a career in nonprofit fundraising and business development, earning the Certified Fund Raising Executive (CFRE) credential and a Juris Master from Florida State University in Legal Risk Management, Contracting, and Compliance. For more than a decade she has worked across major gifts, individual giving, corporate partnerships, and sponsorships: opening doors, building real relationships, and turning conversations into opportunities. It's the same work an athlete's partnerships demand. Know what the other side values, structure the commitment clearly, and steward the relationship so it lasts.
