Four terms to know

Speak the language of the deal.

NIL

Name, image, and likeness: the athlete's identity used in a partnership.

Deliverables

The posts, appearances, or other work promised.

Usage rights

Permission to use the athlete's identity or content: where, how, and for how long.

Exclusivity

A restriction on other partnerships, often by product category or competitor.

Disclosure

Paid or gifted posts must clearly say so, e.g. "Ad" or "Sponsored," and inside the video itself.**
