Dexter Sports Co. LLC, which operates Dexter Sports Management ("we," "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you submit a form or email us, we collect what you choose to share, such as your name, email, phone number, role, sport, organization, and message. Please don't send contract documents, financial account numbers, or other sensitive information through the website.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide requested services or resources.
  • To send occasional updates if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand and improve how the website is used.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted third parties to host this site and process form submissions [e.g., Netlify (hosting and forms), Kit (newsletter), Cloudflare Web Analytics]. They handle information only to provide those services to us.

Analytics & cookies
