Meet the founder

Experience, access, and an advocate's instincts, in your corner.

A Charlotte native and former NCAA Division I volleyball player at Howard University, Kim has lived the student-athlete journey, from junior college to Division I. Since then she has spent more than a decade in fundraising and business development as a Certified Fund Raising Executive (CFRE) with a Juris Master in contracting and compliance from Florida State, building corporate partnerships, sponsorships, and major-gift relationships. Now she brings that deal-making to athletes and coaches.

“The first offer changes the conversation. My job is to make sure the athlete is still leading it.”
