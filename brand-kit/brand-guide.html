Brand guide · v1 · October 2026

Dexter Sports Co.

Coach and athlete representation in every sport, with a specialty in uplifting women. Women-owned, Charlotte, NC. This guide keeps the website, decks, social posts, and printed pieces consistent.

01 · Personality

Protective, plain-spoken, quietly confident.

We sound like

  • A trusted advocate, not a salesperson
  • Plain language: "ask," "understand," "protect"
  • Calm, even about urgent deadlines
  • Athlete- and coach-centered: their goals lead

We never

  • Promise outcomes or dollar results
  • Give legal, tax, or investment advice (we're not a law firm, and we always say so)
  • Use hype, slang, or fear tactics
  • Publish a statistic without a cited source

Taglines: "Protect the talent. Shape the opportunity. Build what lasts." · "Two specialties. One advocate."

The mark is a "D" monogram in a rounded blue square with a white dot. The wordmark sets "Dexter" in Inter Tight ExtraBold and "Sports Co." in Instrument Serif Italic.

Rules

  • Clear space ≥ the height of the "D" on all sides
  • Minimum size: mark 24 px; wordmark 120 px wide
  • Don't recolor, stretch, outline, or add effects
  • Don't place on busy photos without a dark overlay

Interim mark. If Kim commissions a professional logo, replace these files and the site's favicon.svg.

05 · Imagery & motion

Real athletes, real strength.

Home hero: volleyball (Pexels #10350259). Feature women of color in real team jerseys and real action.

  • Women of color in action: strong, focused, powerful, never posed as decoration
  • Real team jerseys and uniforms, not fitness or yoga wear
  • Dark, cinematic backgrounds that blend into Night
  • Natural skin tones; no color washes over people
  • Never digitally alter an athlete's body or face
  • Video: muted, 8–15 s loops, ≤ ~1 MB each after compression
  • Kim's portrait: warm, direct, 4:5 crop
