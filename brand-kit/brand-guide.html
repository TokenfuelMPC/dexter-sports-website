Brand guide · v1 · October 2026

Dexter Sports Co.

Coach and athlete representation in every sport, with a specialty in uplifting women. Women-owned; headquartered in Charlotte, NC, serving clients nationwide. This guide keeps the website, decks, social posts, and printed pieces consistent.

01 · Personality

Protective, plain-spoken, quietly confident.

We sound like

  • A trusted advocate, not a salesperson
  • Plain language: "ask," "understand," "protect"
  • Calm, even about urgent deadlines
  • Athlete- and coach-centered: their goals lead

We never

  • Promise outcomes or dollar results
  • Give legal, tax, or investment advice (we're not a law firm, and we always say so)
  • Use hype, slang, or fear tactics
  • Publish a statistic without a cited source

Taglines: "Protect the talent. Shape the opportunity. Build what lasts." · "Two specialties. One advocate."

03b · Finishes & accents

Blue leads, silver finishes, red whispers.

Liquid silver: the secondary finish

Purpose: a polished, futuristic but classy finish that makes the brand feel premium and distinct from typical agency sites.

  • Matte silver on large surfaces (primary buttons)
  • Shiny mercury on small details: the logo "D", checkmarks, card-icon rims, and the header/card/footer hairlines
  • Headline accent words and big numbers stay blue (blue is dominant)
  • No shiny silver on big areas or body text; it is static (no shimmer animation)

Electric blue: the dominant colour

Purpose: the signature electric-blue light from the original brand. It sits under the silver as a halo, so chrome buttons, numbers, and headline words glow blue.

  • Glows, halos, hero light, the blue NIL card and blue sections
  • Don't replace the blue glow with gray; silver and blue work as a pair

Howard red: a quiet nod

Purpose: honors Kim's alma mater, Howard University, where she played Division I volleyball. It reads as a personal signature, not a brand color.

  • Where it appears: the dot in the logo and favicon; the full stop at the end of headlines; footnote asterisks; every third star in the ticker; the tip of the stat-box accent bar; the hero label marker; the pull-quote rule; the centre of the footer's silver line; the top edge of the "Been in your shoes" card on About
  • Never on buttons, backgrounds, large text, or anything that could be mistaken for an alert
  • Don't confuse it with Alert Red (#E5372A), which is reserved for urgent offers

Real athletes, real strength.

Home hero: volleyball series (Pexels #10350257–#10350262, same athlete), crossfading. Feature women of color in real team jerseys and real action.

  • Real team jerseys and uniforms, not fitness or yoga wear
