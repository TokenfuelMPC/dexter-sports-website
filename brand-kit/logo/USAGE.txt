Dexter Sports Co. logo files
- dexter-mark.svg / dexter-mark-1024.png: the "D" mark (app icons, social avatars, favicons)
- dexter-wordmark-on-dark.*: for dark backgrounds (#07080C)
- dexter-wordmark-on-light.*: for white or light backgrounds (transparent PNG)
Colors: Dexter Blue #0052FF, Night #07080C, Paper #F5F2EB
Fonts: Inter Tight ExtraBold ("Dexter") + Instrument Serif Italic ("Sports Co.") from Google Fonts.
The SVG wordmarks use live text: install the fonts, or convert text to outlines before sending to print.
Rules: keep clear space equal to the "D" height; don't recolor, stretch, or add effects.
