Our approach

Scope matters as much as the number.

Two offers can pay the same and ask for very different things. We look at the full commitment: the work, the rights, the restrictions, and what each one means for what comes next.

See a worked example

Every opportunity, reviewed for

  • Deliverables, approvals, and time commitment
  • Cash vs. products, expenses, and fees
  • Usage rights: where, how long, and paid advertising
  • Exclusivity and impact on future partnerships
  • School, association, and disclosure requirements
  • Cancellation, payment timing, and continuing obligations
